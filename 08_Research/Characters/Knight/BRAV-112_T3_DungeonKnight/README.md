# BRAV-112 — T3 Dungeon Knight concept research

Archived for research review on 2026-09-26. The open-bascinet helmet is
approved; the armour views are retained as research and are not recorded as
final armour approval.

This package contains the current T3 concept set:

- `Knight_DungeonKnight_01_FrontA.png` — front A-pose armour view
- `Knight_DungeonKnight_02_BackA.png` — back A-pose armour view
- `Knight_DungeonKnight_03_ThreeQuarter.png` — three-quarter armour view
- `Knight_DungeonKnight_04_Helm.png` — approved standalone front,
  three-quarter and back helmet studies plus a worn front bust

## Current design direction

- Fitted articulated steel breastplate and backplate with tassets.
- Full arm harness with couters and restrained fitted pauldrons.
- One plain muted-green front cloth panel with no heraldry; no rear banner.
- Brown leather straps, brass buckle and plain wooden belt token.
- Steel greaves and articulated sabatons over brown boots.
- Worn edges and a riveted patched plate provide the repair cue.
- Approved open bascinet has its visor removed, a wide face opening, a short
  mail aventail and full face readability.
- Bare-headed armour views, compact silhouette, no glow, bright gold or
  oversized fantasy armour.

The armour images were generated as identity-preserving equipment edits on the
T2 / BRAV-111 body views. Character identity, body, pose, camera, proportions
and white-background convention were held invariant. A generated rear banner
was removed from the back view to keep the green colour field front-only.

Generated with the built-in image tool. No generator seed was exposed. These
files are concept research inputs only; no 3D generation or rigging is implied
by this archive.
