# BRAV-112 — T2 Man-at-Arms armour concept research

Archived for discussion on 2026-09-25. This package is research material, not
an approved final set.

The package contains the current T2 armour-figure views only:

- `Knight_ManAtArms_01_FrontA.png` — front A-pose
- `Knight_ManAtArms_02_BackA.png` — back A-pose
- `Knight_ManAtArms_03_ThreeQuarter.png` — three-quarter view; specifically
  retained for further discussion

## Current design direction

- Plain munitions-grade steel breastplate and backplate over the quilted
  dark-brown leather jack.
- Short fauld, restrained single shoulder plates, steel vambraces and plain
  greaves over brown boots.
- Muted forest-green wool remains visible at the elbows and hem.
- Wide brown belt, tarnished-brass buckle and wooden token remain part of the
  Knight family language.
- One duller replacement shoulder plate supplies the visible repair cue.
- Bare head, open face, no heraldry, glow, bright gold or oversized armour.

The images were generated as identity-preserving equipment edits on the
accepted BRAV-111/T1 body views. Body, pose, camera, proportions and the white
background convention were held invariant. The back-view T1 mail carryover was
removed for continuity with the front and three-quarter views.

Generated with the built-in image tool. No generator seed was exposed. The T2
kettle helm has not been generated or included. These files are concept
research inputs only; no 3D generation or rigging is implied by this archive.
