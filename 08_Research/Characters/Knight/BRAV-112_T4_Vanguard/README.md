# BRAV-112 — T4 Vanguard armour concept research

Approved by Mustafa and archived as research on 2026-09-26.

This package contains the approved T4 Vanguard concept set:

- `Knight_Vanguard_01_FrontA.png` — front A-pose armour view
- `Knight_Vanguard_02_BackA.png` — back A-pose armour view
- `Knight_Vanguard_03_ThreeQuarter.png` — three-quarter armour view
- `Knight_Vanguard_04_Helm.png` — standalone front, three-quarter and back
  helmet studies plus a worn front bust

## Approved design direction

- Fluted heirloom steel breastplate with a raised rolled rim.
- Restrained fitted pauldrons and a full articulated arm harness.
- Deep muted-green unmarked surcoat panel, brown leather straps and a plain
  wooden belt token.
- Fluted greaves and articulated sabatons over brown boots.
- Thin tarnished old-brass edge lines only; no bright gold.
- A visibly newer replacement strap supplies the repair cue.
- Open-face armet-style helmet with the visor raised and locked above the brow,
  preserving full face readability.
- Bare-headed armour views, restrained silhouette, no heraldry, glow, plume or
  oversized fantasy armour.

The armour images were generated as identity-preserving equipment edits on the
accepted T3 Dungeon Knight / BRAV-111 body views. Character identity, body,
pose, camera, proportions and white-background convention were held invariant.
The helmet was produced as a separate modular concept sheet.

Generated with the built-in image tool. No generator seed was exposed. These
files are approved concept research inputs only; no 3D generation or rigging is
implied by this archive.
