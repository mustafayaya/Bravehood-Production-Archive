# BRAV-112 — T1 Militia concept research

Approved by Mustafa on 2026-09-25.

This package contains the approved T1 Militia armour concept views and the
approved standalone helm sheet for BRAV-112. The armour images were generated
as identity-preserving costume passes on the accepted BRAV-111 body references:

- `Knight_Militia_01_FrontA.png` — BRAV-111 B1 front A-pose target
- `Knight_Militia_02_BackA.png` — BRAV-111 B2 back A-pose target
- `Knight_Militia_03_ThreeQuarter.png` — BRAV-111 B4 three-quarter target
- `Knight_Militia_04_Helm.png` — standalone front / three-quarter / back views

## Design lock

- Quilted dark-brown leather jack with sparse mail at shoulders and elbows.
- Muted forest-green wool underlayer, small plain pauldrons, leather gloves.
- Wide belt, tarnished-brass buckle, wooden token, knee cops and sturdy boots.
- Visible hand repairs; no heraldry, glow, bright gold or oversized armour.
- Armour figure stays bare-headed and preserves the BRAV-111 body, pose,
  camera, proportions and white-background convention.
- Helm is a separate open-face iron skull-cap with nasal, brow band, leather
  liner and a small rear repair plate.

Generated with the built-in image tool. No generator seed was exposed. The
accepted BRAV-111 images are the identity and proportion references for the 3D
step. These are research/source images, not rigged models.
